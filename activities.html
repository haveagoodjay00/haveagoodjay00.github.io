Served 21 months as a short-range guided missile operator. Selected as my unit's Model Soldier (Eutteum Byeongsa), the role given to the soldier with the strongest record and reputation, who is expected to set the standard for the others; this came with the maximum reward leave. Also served as a peer counselor, listening to fellow soldiers and helping them through the difficulties of service life.
