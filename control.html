Root-locus design of a lateral-directional autopilot for the T-50 trainer

Automatic Control term project, Sejong University, fall 2026. A yaw damper and a roll attitude hold loop for a linear T-50 model at 10,000 ft and Mach 0.8, checked against MIL-F-8785C Level 1.

Summary

  • Problem. Open-loop Dutch-roll damping is 0.097, below the 0.19 requirement.
  • Design. Yaw damper (Kr = 8) and roll attitude hold (Kp = 1.44, Kφ = 11.35), chosen by root locus in MATLAB.
  • Finding. The roll loop alone lowers Dutch-roll damping to 0.051. Together with the yaw damper it stays at 0.233.
  • Result. For a 10° bank command: rise time 0.46 s, overshoot 5.4%.
