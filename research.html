Research question

When data center modules are added one at a time to a campus, how do module size and connection structure change N-1 security, margin, losses and the number of modules the network can take? And how does the answer change when the same modules go into an existing area of Seoul or Gyeonggi?

Two cases

  • A. New town
  • B. Existing area of Seoul or Gyeonggi

Four indicators of a balanced design

  • N-1 security
  • Load balance
  • Losses
  • Expansion room
